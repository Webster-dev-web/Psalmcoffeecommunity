Our Story

✧

Psalm Coffee was born from a dream to create more than just a coffee shop. It is a place where people can pause, breathe, and encounter the presence of God in the middle of everyday life.

The vision behind Psalm Coffee is to create a peaceful environment where every person feels welcome, loved, and seen.

Every detail inside Psalm Coffee tells a story of God's love and faithfulness – from the Scripture verses on every coffee cup to the prayer wall, symbolic artwork, and spaces designed to encourage reflection, hope, and community.

Psalm Coffee is not just about serving exceptional coffee; it is about serving people.

Mission

To serve excellent coffee while creating a peaceful space where people can encounter God's love, build meaningful relationships, and find rest for their souls.

Vision

To become a place where faith, community, and hospitality come together, impacting lives one cup of coffee at a time.
